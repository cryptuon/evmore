## Getting Help

### Self-Help

1. **Search this documentation** - Use your browser's find function
2. **Check the [FAQ](getting-started/faq.md)** - Most questions are answered there
3. **Read [Troubleshooting](getting-started/troubleshooting.md)** - Common problems solved
4. **Check the [Glossary](getting-started/glossary.md)** - Understand terms

### Community Support

1. **Discord** - Real-time chat with community members
2. **GitHub Issues** - Report bugs and technical problems
3. **Twitter** - Follow for announcements

### Important Safety Notes

- **Never share your recovery phrase** - No legitimate support will ask for it
- **Verify official links** - Bookmark official sites
- **Be cautious of DMs** - Scammers often impersonate support
